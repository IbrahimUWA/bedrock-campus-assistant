# Personal support | Current Students | Wattlebrook University

Source: https://www.wattlebrook.example/students/support/

## Counselling

The Counselling Service offers free and confidential appointments to enrolled students. You can have up to six sessions each year. Book through the Student Portal or at the Wellbeing Centre in Building 109. Appointments are available from 8.30 am to 4.30 pm on weekdays.

## Campus Safety Team

If you feel unsafe on campus, or are worried about the behaviour of another person, contact the Campus Safety Team on (08) 5550 0142 at any time, day or night. Security officers can walk with you to your car or to the bus station after dark. In an emergency call 000 first.

## AccessAbility Services

AccessAbility Services supports students with a disability, a medical condition or a mental health condition. An adviser prepares an access plan that sets out adjustments for classes, assessments and exams.

## Money and housing

The Student Assistance Fund gives emergency grants of up to 500 dollars to students in unexpected financial hardship. The Housing Office keeps a list of rooms on and near the Riverbend campus.

## Health

The Riverbend Medical Centre in Building 109 has doctors and nurses on weekdays. Appointments are bulk billed for students with a Medicare card.

## Faith and culture

The Multi-faith Centre in Building 112 has prayer rooms open from 7 am to 9 pm every day. The Yarning Place is a study and social space for Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander students.
