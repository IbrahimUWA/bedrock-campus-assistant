# Library | Wattlebrook University

Source: https://www.wattlebrook.example/library/

## Our libraries

- Eleanor Marsh Library (Riverbend campus, Building 105). The main library. Open 24 hours a day during semester for students and staff with an ID card; the service desk is staffed from 8 am to 8 pm on weekdays.
- Harbourside Library (Harbourside campus, Level 2). Open 8 am to 6 pm on weekdays. Closed on weekends.
- Harcourt Special Collections Library (Riverbend campus, Building 200). Holds rare books, maps and the university archives. Open by appointment only; book at least two working days ahead.

## Borrowing

Undergraduate students can borrow up to 30 items for four weeks. Postgraduate students and staff can borrow up to 60 items for eight weeks. Loans renew automatically unless another person has requested the item. There are no fines for late returns, but you cannot borrow again until a recalled item is returned.

## Study spaces

Group study rooms can be booked for up to two hours a day through the library website. The top floor of the Eleanor Marsh Library is a silent study area.

## Help with research

Faculty librarians offer 30-minute consultations on finding sources, referencing and managing research data. Book through the library website.
