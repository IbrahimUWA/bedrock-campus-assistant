# How to apply | Domestic students | Wattlebrook University

Source: https://www.wattlebrook.example/study/apply/domestic/

## Undergraduate courses

1. Choose a course and check its entry requirements, including the minimum selection rank and any prerequisite subjects.
2. If you are finishing Year 12 this year, apply through the State Admissions Centre and list Wattlebrook courses in your preferences.
3. If you are not a current school leaver, apply directly to the university through the Apply Online portal. There is no application fee for direct applications.
4. Upload certified copies of your academic transcripts and proof of citizenship or residency.
5. Accept your offer by the date shown in your offer letter, then enrol in your units.

## Postgraduate courses

Apply directly through the Apply Online portal. Research degrees also need a short research proposal and the name of a supervisor who has agreed to work with you.

## Closing dates

- Semester 1 2027: direct applications close on Friday 29 January 2027.
- Semester 2 2027: direct applications close on Friday 9 July 2027.

Some courses with limited places, including Physiotherapy and Veterinary Science, close earlier, on 30 September of the year before.

## Other ways in

If you do not meet the entry requirements, the Wattlebrook Pathway Program is a one-semester enabling course. Completing it with a credit average guarantees a place in most bachelor degrees.
