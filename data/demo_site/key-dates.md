## Alternative exam arrangements

If you have a disability, a medical condition or carer responsibilities, you can ask for adjustments such as extra time, a separate room or rest breaks. Register with AccessAbility Services first; they prepare an access plan and send it to the Exams Office. Applications received after the last date above cannot be guaranteed for that exam period.

## Deferred exams

You can apply for a deferred exam if illness or another circumstance outside your control stopped you from sitting an exam. Apply through the Student Portal within five working days of the exam and attach supporting documents, such as a medical certificate. A deferred exam is not a second attempt: you cannot apply after you have sat the exam.

## On the day

Bring your student ID card. Arrive 20 minutes before the start time. You will not be admitted more than 30 minutes after an exam has started.
