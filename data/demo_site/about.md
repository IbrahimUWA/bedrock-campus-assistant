# About us | Wattlebrook University

Source: https://www.wattlebrook.example/about/

Wattlebrook University is a fictional public university created for this demonstration. Nothing on these pages describes a real institution.

## At a glance

- Founded in 1974 as the Wattlebrook Institute of Technology, and granted university status in 1991.
- About 18,400 students, of whom roughly 3,100 are postgraduates.
- Two campuses: the main Riverbend campus and the smaller Harbourside campus in the city.
- Four faculties: Science and Engineering, Health, Business and Law, and Arts and Education.

## Rankings and reputation

The Global Campus Index 2026 places Wattlebrook in the top 250 universities in the world. In the same index the university is ranked 31st in the world for mineral and mining engineering, its highest subject ranking. The National Graduate Outcomes Survey 2025 reported that 89 per cent of Wattlebrook bachelor graduates were in full-time work within four months of finishing.

## Research

Wattlebrook has three research institutes: the Institute for Dryland Agriculture, the Centre for Remote Sensing, and the Coastal Health Research Unit. Research income in 2025 was 96 million dollars.

## Leadership

The Vice-Chancellor is Professor Imogen Tallis, who took up the role in February 2023. The Chancellor is Dr Rafael Okoro.

## Visiting

The Riverbend campus is open to visitors from 7 am to 9 pm on weekdays. Guided campus tours run every Wednesday at 10 am during semester and leave from the Welcome Centre in Building 100.
