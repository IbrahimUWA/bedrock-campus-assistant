# Fees | Current Students | Wattlebrook University

Source: https://www.wattlebrook.example/students/fees/

## Your fee statement

Your fee statement is issued in the Student Portal two weeks before each study period starts. It lists the tuition fee for each unit and the Student Services and Amenities Fee.

## How to pay

- Online with PayPoint, the university's payment page, using a credit or debit card. Card payments have a surcharge of 0.9 per cent.
- By BPAY from your bank account, using the biller code and reference number printed on your fee statement. There is no surcharge.
- In person at a post office, by showing the barcode on your fee statement.

The university does not accept cash or cheques on campus.

## Due dates

Fees are due by the census date of each study period. For Semester 1 2027 the census date is Wednesday 31 March 2027. For Semester 2 2027 it is Tuesday 31 August 2027.

## If you pay late

A late fee of 150 dollars is added seven days after the due date. If fees are still unpaid 28 days after the due date, your enrolment for that study period may be cancelled.

## Payment plans

If you cannot pay in full, you can ask for a payment plan before the due date. A plan splits the amount into up to four instalments across the study period. Apply through the Student Portal under Finance.

## Refunds

If you withdraw from a unit on or before the census date, the tuition fee for that unit is refunded in full.
